Show your keys on screen while you play.

A 3D keyboard and mouse that sit on top of your game and light up the instant you press. It works with sounds off, too.

  • Pick your keycaps. Eight colourways and four keycap shapes, or your own colours.
  • Click to choose keys. WASD, a gaming half, the full board, or exactly the keys you click.
  • Put the mouse anywhere. Move and size the keyboard and mouse separately.
  • Only where you want it. Show it over Fortnite only, everywhere, or follow your sound rules. Ctrl + Alt + O hides it mid-game.

Put it on your stream.

Streamer mode hands OBS the exact same overlay, with a transparent background. It can be on stream without being on your own screen.

  1. Turn on Streamer modeOn ZeroClick's Overlay page.
  2. Copy a linkKeyboard and mouse, keyboard only, or mouse only, like http://127.0.0.1:47800/all
  3. Add it to OBSSources, then +, then Browser. Paste the link and set the size ZeroClick shows you.

How fast can you click?

ZeroClick has a 10-second click test. Turn on the leaderboard in the app, create an account, and your best score shows up here. Log in on any PC to keep your name and score.

Built to be fast.

Native, not a web page

Most sound apps run inside a hidden browser. ZeroClick is a small native Windows app that talks straight to Windows' low-latency audio engine.

Sound in milliseconds

Every sound is loaded into memory and trimmed of the silence many packs have baked in. On a typical PC a key press reaches the mixer in about 5 ms, and ZeroClick shows you its measured number.

Never touches your game

It reads your keyboard and mouse the way any Windows app can, and draws its overlay in its own window. Nothing is injected into the game.

Questions

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is that normal?

Yes. ZeroClick isn't signed with a paid certificate yet, so Windows SmartScreen doesn't recognise it. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Is it safe with Fortnite's anti-cheat?

ZeroClick doesn't touch the game at all: it never injects anything or reads game memory. It listens to your keyboard and mouse like any other Windows app and plays sound from its own process.

Does the overlay work in fullscreen?

It works in Windowed Fullscreen and Borderless. Nothing can draw over exclusive Fullscreen without hooking into the game, so ZeroClick can switch Fortnite to Windowed Fullscreen for you in one click. On Windows 10 and 11 it usually plays just as smoothly.

Can I use my own sounds?

Yes. Drop a Mechvibes pack (a folder or .zip) onto ZeroClick, or record your own keyboard and drop the recording in. ZeroClick splits it into separate clicks for you.

How does it update?

ZeroClick checks for new versions on its own and shows a small popup. Click Update and it restarts on the new version.

How do I uninstall it?

Open Windows Settings, go to Apps, find ZeroClick in the list and choose Uninstall. You can choose to keep your settings in case you come back.

Does it cost anything?

No. ZeroClick is free.
